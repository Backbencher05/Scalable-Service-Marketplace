# Marketplace Business Model

## Vision

Build a scalable service marketplace platform that connects customers with service providers and enables reliable scheduling, booking, payment, and service delivery.

The platform is inspired by:

- Urban Company
- Fiverr
- Calendly

---

# Core Problem

Customers need trusted professionals to perform services.

Providers need a reliable way to discover customers and grow their business.

The marketplace acts as a trusted intermediary that coordinates both sides.

---

# Marketplace Participants

## Customer

The customer wants:

- Service discovery
- Easy booking
- Reliable providers
- Secure payments
- Transparent pricing

---

## Provider

The provider wants:

- Customer acquisition
- Booking management
- Schedule management
- Earnings visibility
- Reputation growth

---

## Platform

The platform wants:

- Successful bookings
- Customer satisfaction
- Provider retention
- Revenue generation
- Marketplace trust

---

# Marketplace Value Proposition

The platform creates value by:

1. Matching customers with providers.
2. Coordinating scheduling and availability.
3. Managing bookings.
4. Facilitating payments.
5. Building trust through reviews and verification.

---

# Revenue Model

Potential revenue sources:

- Commission per booking
- Provider subscription plans
- Featured provider listings
- Premium marketplace services

---

# Key Insight

The marketplace is not merely a listing platform.

The marketplace is fundamentally a:

> Trust and Coordination System

Its primary responsibility is to ensure that customers and providers can successfully complete service transactions with confidence.
